About me

Jonathan Pilnick.

I’m a builder at heart. I take ambitious ideas in AI, markets and products and turn them into systems that work in the real world.

Now. I research AI reasoning — new frameworks that help models reason better while staying aligned and safe. The work is mentored by two professors through my university’s Academic Excellence Research Program, and a provisional US patent has been filed.

Building. For 5+ years I’ve co-founded and led research on an automated algorithmic trading system. I also built Smart Study, a productivity app that reached 150,000+ users, which is now growing into Thrive, an AI scheduling app.

Service. In the Israeli Air Force I founded first-of-their-kind AI systems with its elite AI unit, led an innovation team in the IAF accelerator, and worked with international defense partners.

Roots. Born in Israel, raised in California, finished high school in Barcelona, and back in Israel since 2021. B.Sc. in Computer Science (deep learning), class of 2026. I speak English, Hebrew and Spanish.
