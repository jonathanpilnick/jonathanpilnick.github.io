About me

Jonathan Pilnick.

I’m a builder at heart. I take ambitious ideas and use technology to turn them into systems that work in the real world.

I have immense motivation to succeed in everything I do. I strive for excellence, look for feedback, am not afraid to fail, and seek to work alongside top performers I can learn from, so I can improve quickly.

Now

I’m working on independent AI research. The motivation is to make future AI more interpretable, reliable, traceable and aligned, by better representing its internal reasoning signals within the neural network. A provisional US patent has been filed.

Building

For as long as I can remember, creating and innovating have been a central part of who I am. At age 8 I would compose music on my electronic keyboard all day long, mixing several instruments. At age 10 I was obsessed with modelling reality in Blender, creating objects and scenes and rendering their animation.

As I grew, I realized that although I had many ideas and immense motivation to make them happen, I was limited by and dependent on others. That realization led me to where I am today: I had to become technically proficient in many fields to make things happen.

Over the years, it led me to build my first large project, Thrive, which I scaled to over 150k users — and from there to algorithmic trading research, AI systems in the Israeli Air Force, and my own independent AI research.

Roots

Born in Israel, raised in California, completed the IB Diploma Programme in Barcelona, and back in Israel since 2021. B.Sc. in Computer Science (deep learning), class of 2026. I speak English and Hebrew natively, and a bit of Spanish.
